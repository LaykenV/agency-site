# Client research

This directory holds client- and prospect-specific research that should not be
mixed into the business plan, growth strategy, or system architecture.

Research may contain public contact details, captured pricing, assumptions, and
dated vendor information. Treat it as private operational material:

- date facts and sources
- distinguish verified facts from estimates
- recheck prices, law, vendor terms, and prospect status before using them
- move durable business decisions into the relevant canonical document
- do not present a research price band as an accepted quote
